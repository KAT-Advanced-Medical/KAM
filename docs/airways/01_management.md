# Airways Management & Interventions

## Immediate Diagnostics: Checking Breathing
*Action performed on the Head interaction menu.*

| Result | Indication |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Normal** | No immediate airway issues present. |
| **Shallow** | Pneumothorax (PTX) signature. |
| **None** | Cardiac Arrest, complete occlusion/obstruction, or advanced Hemothorax/TPTX. |

## Airway Status & Clear Procedures
Always manage airways from most protective status down to functional preservation.

* **Occluded Status**
  * *Treatment:* Use Accuvac, Manual Suction Pump, Head Turning, or transition to Recovery Position. Repeat until successful.
* **Obstructed Status**
  * *Treatment:* Perform Head Hyperextending or transition to Recovery Position. Stay within close proximity to the patient during performance.
* **Clear Status (Protection)**
  * *Treatment:* Insert King LT (Best protection), Guedel Tube (Moderate protection), or secure in Recovery Position.

---

## The Recovery Position
Secures the airway from future occlusion or obstruction events but locks or prevents specific local medical procedures.

### Requirements for Action Appearance
* Patient must be Unconscious
* Not currently in a recovery position
* No Guedel Tube or King LT inserted
* Patient is not inside a vehicle or on a stretcher

### Restricted Interventions (While Position Active)
The position must be canceled (via Carrying, Dragging, or loading into a vehicle) to execute:
* Guedel Tube / King LT / Accuvac insertion
* Chest Seals or AAT/NCD Kit applications
* Head Overstretch / Turning
* AED Analysis / Shocks & CPR
* FAST IO placement
